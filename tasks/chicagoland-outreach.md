## 1. Decision for Henry: the Illinois registration sticker

**Illinois still uses a registration sticker, on the upper-right corner of the REAR
plate.**
- 625 ILCS 5/3-413(k): stickers "shall be displayed on the upper right corner of the
  rear registration plate".
- 625 ILCS 5/3-413(b): stickers must be "clearly visible at all times".
- Source for both: the statute text on ilga.gov, read 2026-10-11, including the
  amendments effective 2026 and 2027. Neither amendment removes stickers for ordinary
  plates; P.A. 104-125 adds digital stickers for digital plates only.

**What we know:**
- Our frame's 0.75" top runner and 0.5" right side **probably** reach into that
  corner. How far depends on the sticker's size and position on the 2017 plate, which
  we have not measured.
- The top runner covers most of the word "ILLINOIS". The keystone tab probably covers
  the middle of "LAND OF LINCOLN", which carries lower risk because the slogan is not
  registration data.
- **Illinois also requires a FRONT plate**, and the front plate carries no sticker.

**Case law and tolls:**
- *United States v. Hernandez Flores*, 7th Cir. No. 15-1515 (2015), as summarised by the
  Wisconsin State Public Defender: a frame that partly covered the region name on a
  plate did not violate 3-413(b), because frames "generally surround the periphery of
  the plate". Read the opinion before relying on it; it does not address a hidden
  sticker.
- 605 ILCS 10/27.2 (Toll Highway Act) bans devices that "obstruct the visibility or
  electronic image recording of the plate" on toll roads: $750 and confiscation. It is
  **untested** whether a decorative frame over the sticker counts. It is worth naming
  because I-94 and I-294 are the North Shore commute.

**Options:**

| | What it is | Cost | Risk left |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Front-plate frame in Illinois** | Sell it as the front-plate frame in IL; say so on the IL builder and the order email | Copy only, no geometry | State name still covered (same as MO) |
| **B. Illinois variant** | Clear the sticker corner: notch or thin the top-right of the runner | A geometry variant + Bill's part change + a test print | Needs the real sticker size first |
| **C. Ship as-is + counsel** | Same frame as Missouri | None | The sticker question stays open |

**Recommendation: measure a real Illinois rear plate first.** That means the sticker's
size and its distance from the top and right edges; ten minutes with a tape. If the
frame clears the sticker, the question disappears. If it does not, A costs nothing,
and B is the fix if parents want the rear plate. Either way, the counsel check already
planned for Missouri should cover Illinois at the same time.

## 2. What the market looks like

- **No school-specific plate frame was found at any of the 30.** This is an absence in
  our searches, not proof.
- **Car items are ALREADY sold by several parent groups.** Pitch the frame as one more
  product for their store, with $5 a frame to them, not as a rival:

| School | What they sell | Source |
|---|---|---|
| New Trier Booster Club | Calls itself the "sole provider" of athletic yard signs and magnets | newtrier.k12.il.us booster pages; wording on the older page |
| ETHS Boosters | Car magnets for any activity | ethsboosters.org/wildkit-merchandise |
| GBN Boosters | Shows a car sticker | booster site |
| Loyola Academy store | Has a "Car Magnets and Decals" category | shop.goramblers.org |
| Naperville Central Boosters | Car decals | nchsboosters.com |

- **Approvals.** Illinois districts commonly adopt the IASB model policy 8:90, which
  requires the superintendent's WRITTEN consent before a parent group uses a school's
  name or logo. Wilmette D39 and D41 publish it that way; we did not read the 30
  schools' own districts' policies.
  - That consent is the same written permission our `verified` flip already requires.
  - Catholic schools guard their crest: expect the school, and possibly the religious
    order, to be the gate.
- **IHSA marks are not involved.** IHSA's trademark policy covers IHSA's own marks
  (IHSA handbook); school logos belong to the schools.

## 4. Product notes from the research

- **New Trier is two federal rows**: its Northfield freshman campus is listed
  separately. Kits now have `campusRosterIds`, and the campus's own URL redirects to
  the one builder (tested).
- **Loyola Academy and Carmel Catholic are not in the federal private-school data**,
  like CBC.
  - Their builders work.
  - The national roster search cannot find them; they appear only in the authored list.
- **Missing badges.** Badminton has state titles at New Trier, Neuqua (2025) and
  Stevenson (all Wikipedia/NCTV17). Mock trial has Hinsdale Central's 11 ISBA titles.
  Both would be good Illinois library additions.
- **Scholastic Bowl** has state titles at Stevenson and Hinsdale Central (Wikipedia).
  Its art is still withheld (the desk-bell problem), so redrawing it pays twice. The
  "scholastic bowl" chip alias is already mapped.
- **Colours.**
  - Every colour was measured from the school's own logo where one could be fetched,
    and cross-checked against the site's theme CSS.
  - Each `colorSource` says which, and flags where the school's own reds or blues
    disagree: Benet, Mount Carmel, Highland Park, GBS, LT and Marist.
  - Where no school colour can outline white lettering (white-and-red, white-and-navy
    and black-and-white schools), the rim is a neutral silver `#A7A9AC` rather than
    the renderer's brass fallback, which Henry called off-brand on Ladue.
- **Illinois preview plate.** IL builders show a drawn Illinois plate, not a
  photograph. Missouri has a blurred real one (`SCHOOL_STOCK_PLATES`). An IL stock
  photo is worth doing before any Chicago school opens.
